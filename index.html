Core Contribution

PhD Thesis: Cross-Layer GPU Information Leakage

My thesis examines information leakage across three abstraction layers—microarchitectural resources, interconnect fabrics (NVLink), and application-level LLM inference—demonstrating that no single mitigation eliminates all channels.

🔬 Microarchitectural Layer: 44.7 Mbps covert channel

Reverse-engineered warp scheduling on NVIDIA A100; 10x improvement over prior work. Randomization reduces ML detectability to 36%.

🔗 Interconnect Layer: NVLink attack surface

First to expose NVLink across V100/A100/H200. Covert channel: 6.33–9.90 Kbps. Side-channel fingerprinting: 96.2% accuracy (25 apps).

🤖 Application Layer: LLM inference leakage

Model fingerprinting: 99.94% accuracy (7 LLMs). Prompt-family inference: 81–100% accuracy under realistic stochastic decoding.
